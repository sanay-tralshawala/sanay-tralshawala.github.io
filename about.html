About Me

I'm a graduate student at Carnegie Mellon University pursuing a Master of Science in Artificial Intelligence Engineering with a concentration in Robotics & Controls. With a strong foundation in mechanical engineering and a passion for AI, I focus on solving complex problems at the intersection of perception, autonomy, and controls. I have previously completed a Bachelor of Science in Mechanical Engineering at Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute, where I developed a broad skill set in engineering principles, design, and analysis. My academic and research experiences have equipped me with expertise in computer vision, machine learning, robotics, and engineering design.

Current Focus

My research at the Computational Engineering and Robotics Lab (CERLAB) centers on Spatial AI and Vision Foundation models for Simultaneous Localization and Mapping (SLAM) systems. I'm particularly interested in how advanced computer vision can improve object re-identification and robustness in autonomous systems.

Expertise & Interests

  • Engineering: Computer-Aided Design, structural analysis, thermodynamic systems, Probabilistic Risk Assessment (PRA)
  • Technical Skills: Python, MATLAB, C++, Solidworks, TensorFlow, PyTorch
