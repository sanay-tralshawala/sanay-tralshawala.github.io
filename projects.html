Projects

Trajectory Prediction as Forward Reachability for Airport Surface Safety

Embodied Artificial Intelligence Safety

Proactive runway incursion detection ahead of conflict: Adapted calibrated forward reachable sets to airport surface operations using Amelia-TF trajectory forecasts, aircraft-specific kinematic feasibility weighting, and conformal calibration for statistical coverage guarantees.

Semantic Segmentation of Satellite Imagery

Deep Learning

Compared architectures for aerial land-cover mapping: Benchmarked ResNet34 U-Net, ConvNeXt-Tiny, and DINOv3 ConvNeXt-Tiny on the FLAIR-1 dataset. Conducted channel ablation with Near Infrared and elevation inputs and applied conformal prediction for uncertainty quantification.

Agentic Bus Routing from Disruption Data

AI Agents · Natural Language Processing

Built an LLM pipeline to reroute buses from unstructured news: Agentic framework for bus route planning under dynamic events such as road disruptions. Designed the News Parser Agent with a two-stage classification and extraction pipeline, implemented confidence scoring for operator review thresholds, and orchestrated the full end-to-end system.

Visualization and 3D Modeling of Microwave Food

Computer Vision Engineering

Generated 3D thermal models to optimize cooking: Combined thermal imaging, color detection, and depth data using frame subtraction and ICP fusion to create temperature-annotated 3D models of rotating food. Explored NeRF and stereo depth approaches for mesh generation.

Power Demand Prediction Based on Weather Data

Machine Learning Engineering

Developed predictive models for energy load forecasting: Analyzed regional weather and power load data. Built LSTM based RNN models and Random Forest models with hyperparameter optimization to forecast energy demand patterns. This included clustered models based on K-means clustering and seasons.

Wing Spar Optimization for Aircraft Design

Numerical Design Optimization

Minimized the structural mass of a UAV carbon-fiber wing spar using gradient-based optimization and Euler-Bernoulli finite element analysis. Extended the model to optimization under uncertainty by applying stochastic collocation (Gauss-Hermite quadrature), enforcing a six-sigma stress constraint creating more realistic structural deflections.
